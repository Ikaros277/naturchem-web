# NATURCHEM, s.r.o.

> NATURCHEM is a Czech accredited testing laboratory No. 1599 (CAI, ČSN EN ISO/IEC 17025). It provides environmental measurements, studies, expert reports and permitting documentation for operators, investors and designers across the Czech Republic.

## Service scope

Services provided in the Czech Republic:

- accredited / authorized emission measurement (boilers, stacks, VOC/TOC, particulates, NOx, CO)
- workplace environment measurement and job categorization
- noise measurement, noise studies, acoustic reports
- dispersion study for permits or EIA
- expert report under the Air Protection Act
- operating rules for emission sources
- EIA documentation, IPPC, ISPOP, GHG calculations, emissions data checks and carbon footprint
- environmental contractor with documentation for KHS, ČIŽP and regional authorities
